Six workflows around one Airtable base

The scraper saves each role to Airtable and posts the best fits to Slack. If the AI call fails, the roles are still saved, unscored. The other five workflows read the same base, so nothing is copied by hand.

What n8n reports

n8n's own insights for 14 to 21 November 2026 show about 765 production runs, of which 4 failed. That is a 0.5% failure rate, with an average run of 27.6 seconds. The scraper alone runs every 15 minutes.

What four of the workflows do

Five of the six are published and one is partly published. The AI model is Groq, and nothing goes to an employer without me.

  • Scraper

    Scores each new role and drafts a proposal.

  • Reply tracker

    Matches Gmail replies to leads. Never sets Interview itself.

  • Interview prep

    Writes a one-page brief for each interview.

  • Follow-ups

    Drafted each morning. I send them.
