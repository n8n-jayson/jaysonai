Import, audit, then publish

Every imported workflow is opened and checked against one list before it is switched on. What passes is published. What has not been checked yet stays a draft, and a replaced version is renamed and kept.

What the accounts show

Two newer client accounts each hold about 135 imported workflows. On 21 November 2026 one had 5 published and the other 18. All four accounts checked that day read no workflow errors found.

What the audit checks

I run it on new client accounts as a build specialist for a US home-services marketing agency. The checks are written up as an onboarding playbook.

  • Links and webhooks

    Pointed at this client's pages and endpoints.

  • Calendars and users

    Set to this client's calendars and team.

  • Alerts and messages

    Name the right owner and no other business.

  • Custom values

    Each one a workflow reads exists and is filled in.
