Where leads leak

Most businesses don't need more leads. They need to answer the ones they have.

Leak 1

Slow first reply

An enquiry lands after hours and sits until morning. By then the lead has messaged two competitors.

Fix: an instant reply on WhatsApp, SMS, Messenger or web chat that asks the right questions.

Leak 2

Missed calls

Most callers who reach voicemail never leave a message. They call the next number on the list.

Fix: a text-back within seconds and a callback list sorted by urgency.

Leak 3

No follow-up

Leads rarely say no. They go quiet, and nobody has time to chase them for weeks.

Fix: a nurture sequence that keeps going and stops the moment they reply or book.
