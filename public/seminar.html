The registration workflow: four emails, three texts

Each registrant gets a confirmation and a first text, then reminders on fixed dates: seven days before, two days before, the day before, that morning and three hours before. The office is alerted on every sign-up.

What the records show

The main workflow's log for 23 October to 21 November 2026 shows no failed steps. Sign-ups passed the room's capacity, nine cancellations freed their seats, and after the event 19 people who attended and 12 who did not went into their follow-ups.

What I changed in the seminar funnel

Three of the seven workflows existed when I started as the agency's operations manager. I reworked the main one and added four.

  • Fixed dates

    Each reminder waits for a date, not a count of days.

  • Waitlist

    Moving someone off it starts their confirmation and reminders.

  • Cancellations

    A cancelled tag stops the reminders and frees the seats.

  • Next

    One seat path with the count in a field, not three copies.
